## Scope

Two pieces Jake designed and approved on 2026-11-01 through mock-ups at real length and his own data:
[the five pills](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-personal-view-four-tabs-design.md) (the 2026-10-31 fold of Performance into Records made
Progress 9,274 px long and buried the 1M to All range) and
[Lifts and Blocks](../superpowers/plans/2026-11-01-performance-lifts-and-blocks.md) (first piece of the
[performance charts design](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-performance-charts-design.md)).

**In:** Personal view's Progress page and the Performance page it opens, for Personal view, a coached client's own Progress page and
the shared stats card. **Out:** the coach's client-profile tab, merging lift names, Sessions "vs last time", weekly volume, cardio over
time, the weight-plausibility check (all designed, not built).

---

## What shipped

Plain English first.
- **Progress has five pills** in Personal view: **Goals · Records · Performance · Bodyweight · Cardio**, in one small row that never
  scrolls sideways (two rows on phones under 390 px wide). Records is short again (1,388 px, was 9,274); Performance is its own page.
- **Performance's pills are Lifts · Sessions · Blocks.** A saved old tab name still opens the right one.
- **Lifts say what they show:** a plain title for the measure ("Estimated 1-rep max"), a one-line explainer that says which way is
  good, "+23 kg since 12 Aug", and the best point marked and named under the chart. A lift with one session says so.
- **Blocks opens on "this block vs last block":** each lift's best estimated 1-rep max then and now with the change in kg and %; a lift
  in only one block says "not trained this block" or "new this block" and never shows a change. With no plan running it compares the
  last 6 weeks with the 6 before. A plan not started yet is ignored; two plans running at once never count a day in both blocks.
  Tapping a row opens that lift in the week-by-week overlay below, on the same measure.
- Fixed on the way: Performance's own row of pills no longer scrolls sideways; two Performance renders in a row no longer both fill the page.

Technical: `_PERSONAL_PROGRESS_TABS`, `_personalProgressTab`, `.chip-row-fit`/`.chip-row-3`; `_PERF_TABS`, `_perfTabName`;
`_blockWindowsFor`, `_blockLiftSummary`, `_blockSummaryHtml`, `_pickBlockLift`; `_xsPlainFacts` and `opts.plain` on the shared card
(the runner's Stats sheet does not opt in); `_renderMetricChart` keeps a bigger series' point size when selected.
