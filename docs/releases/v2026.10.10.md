## What shipped

Plain English first. **On your phone:** open the site, choose Add to Home Screen, and CoachApp gets its own icon (the "C" mark)
and opens like an app. Nothing about how the app works changes. **For coaches:** Home now starts with "Needs attention": missed
planned sessions, gone quiet, a plan ending or ended with nothing booked, an overdue check-in, no recent weigh-in, ranked most
urgent first. A holiday mutes the training reasons.

Technical (installable app):
- `manifest.webmanifest`: `standalone`, `start_url` and `scope` `./` (works under `/coachapp/`), dark `#11131a`, three icons.
- `icons/`: 192, 512, a 512 maskable (the C inside Android's safe zone) and a 180 px iPhone icon, drawn from the existing mark by
  `scripts/make-icons.mjs` (Playwright renders Inter; it refuses a fallback font).
- `index.html`: the manifest and iPhone icon links, `mobile-web-app-capable`, `apple-mobile-web-app-capable`, title, and a
  "black" status bar (not translucent: the app does not pad for content under the status bar).
- Both local servers serve `.webmanifest`; `scripts/verify-live.mjs` also compares the icons the manifest lists (and skips any
  `..` path). `scripts/release.mjs` and `scripts/lib/review-fingerprint.mjs` now treat the manifest, icons, `privacy-policy.html`,
  `404.html` and `.claude/launch.json` as code: a change to them after the suite run or the review invalidates both.

Technical (Needs attention): see `da57a81`'s message — `ATTENTION_RULES`, `_attentionFor` and `_attentionRank` in
`js/app-core.js`; three new paged reads in `renderDashboard`; honest failure states.

  no plan) — named in its commit.

## Rollback

Tag `v2026.10.9`'s commit and push it, or revert the range. Nothing changes stored data or the database. A phone that installed
the app keeps its home-screen icon; after a rollback it opens the site in the browser's full-screen mode only if the manifest
is still served, otherwise as an ordinary page.
