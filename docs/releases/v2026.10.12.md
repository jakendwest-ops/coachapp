## What shipped

Plain English first. In Personal view the bottom bar and the sidebar now have four places to go:
- **Today** (opens first): today's session, or on a rest day "Train anyway" with the next session; the week; a **Calendar** button
  (the calendar has a "‹ Today" link back); log today's **body weight** right on the card; goals, with "See all" to Progress › Goals.
- **Train**: **Workouts** (first) and **Plans**.
- **Progress**: **Goals** (first), **Records** (your 1RMs, your other records, and Per exercise / Per session / Per training plan),
  **Weight**, **Cardio** (your cardio records on their own).
- **More**: Exercise library, Settings, Download my data, Send feedback, Privacy policy, Switch to PT view, Sign out.
Old links and saved pages (`#library`, `#calendar`, `#goals`, `#programs`) land on their new homes.

Also fixed on the way: the Weight page accepted a weight of 0 or below (now refused); closing the workout editor in Personal view
went to the exercise list (now your workouts); a double tap on the new weight Log can't save twice.

Technical: `_NAV_ITEMS.solo` = 4 items, `_soloNavParent` lights a retired page's parent tab, `_isPersonalView()`;
`_trainSwitchHtml`; solo `renderWorkoutLibrary` = Exercise library only; `_dashQuickWeight` + shared `_insertWeightLog`
(ownership-checked, refuses <= 0); `renderPersonalProgress` (the client keeps `renderProgress`'s four tabs);
`renderClientPerformance(…, { categories })`; `_perfHost`; CSS `.tap-link`, `.more-row`, `.dash-quickweight`.
