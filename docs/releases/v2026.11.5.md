## Scope

Fix the high-priority bug Jake reported on 2026-11-14: "Update all copies" made every week's copy of a workout match the edited one, wiping each week's own progressed loads ([row](../bugs/2026-11-14-update-all-copies-overwrites-each-copy-instead-of-applying-only-the-changes.md)). ## What shipped

- **Save asks what to copy.** After editing a workout that has copies in other weeks, Save offers **Only my changes** (the default: a changed rest reaches only the copies' rest; each copy keeps its own weights, reps and effort) or **Whole workout** (an exact copy, warned in red). The dialogue names what changed, for example "Bench: rest" or "Row -> Pendlay Row".
- **Only this week and later.** Editing Week 3 no longer reaches Weeks 1-2, in programmes and client plans. The dialogue says how many earlier copies were left alone; a copy whose week cannot be read is held back, never guessed.
- **Opening and closing an exercise without changing it no longer counts as an edit** (a difference in stored key order used to).

Technical: `js/app-workouts.js`: `_exerciseEdits` / `_applyExerciseEdits` (field-level edits), `_replaceWholeWorkoutInTemplates` (insert-then-delete with a guarded rollback), `_canonJson` for change detection, `_checkSiblingPropagation` filters copies by phase `order_index` then `week_number`. Specs: `tests/update-copies-choice-2026-11-14.spec.js` (red on the old code, green now), `tests-node/exercise-edits.test.mjs`; `tests/reentry-guard-2026-08-28.spec.js` adjusted.
