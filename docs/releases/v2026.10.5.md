## Scope

Three things Jake asked for or approved on 2026-10-16/17: the program page's week actions on the page instead of behind a ⋯ menu; the Personal account's Workouts page reworked (his four points: the plan first, "Create a workout" with two choices, a search by name, Edit in place with a lock for the plan you are on); and the UTC-date fix he had left waiting on his go. The review of the date fix found two database leftovers and a display twin, so those were fixed in the same release.

**In:**
- **Program page:** **Duplicate week** and **Delete week** are two buttons under the week tabs (this week only); the week's ⋯ menu is gone. Confirmed by Jake ("looks good!").
- **Workouts page (Personal account only):**
  - the plan block (Up next, the folded plan) comes first;
  - **Create a workout** replaces "Start an empty workout" and offers **Choose a template** (My workouts saves a "Copy of …"; Past sessions saves a workout of that session's exercises) or **Create new** (a name, then the existing editor; both save at once);
  - **Train now** is a small link for a blank live session; "Start from a past session" is gone;
  - a **search by name** over Your workouts (20 at a time, "Show more"; it reads the newest 100 and says so);
  - **Edit** and **Make a copy** on each row; a workout in the plan the account is on NOW has no Edit (it names the plan and offers "Open in Training plans"; Make a copy still works); a finished or removed plan unlocks its workouts.
- **"Today" is the local day, not the UTC day:** 13 places (the session's date, the three 1RM saves, a plan block's end date, the weight, PB, log-session, add-event and add-to-plan forms, the data download's file name); six screens that show a stored date no longer show the day before west of UTC (`_dateOnly`); a restarted plan's block ends on the day you are on (`assign_program` takes `p_today`); the coach summary counts a session dated one day ahead of the UTC date (it was hidden for the first hour after midnight in the UK summer).
- **Tests and checks:** three count ratchets keep the UTC-day expression and the UTC-midnight display parse out; 48 spec fixtures moved to the local date; eight specs wait for the dashboard before they open anything.

**Deliberately out:**

- A shared spec helper for the solo start (three specs with a different start were left; named in `docs/technical-debt.md`).

## What shipped

Plain English first. **Workouts page:** if you are on a plan it is at the top; below it "Create a workout" gives two choices, a template or a new one, and either way the workout is saved straight away and opens in the editor. Your workouts has a search box and each row has Edit and Make a copy; a workout that belongs to the plan you are on right now cannot be edited here (it says which plan and takes you to it), but you can copy it and edit the copy. **Program page:** Duplicate week and Delete week are buttons under the week tabs. **Dates:** a session or form that says "today" now means the day on your clock; in the UK summer that fixes the first hour after midnight, and for anyone west of UTC it fixes the whole evening (a session logged at 8 pm in New York was filed under tomorrow).

Technical: `js/app-workouts.js` (the pure rules `_wkWeekless`, `_wkPlanFinished`, `_wkRows`, `_wkMatch`; `renderClientWorkoutsPage`, `_yourWorkoutsHtml`, `_wkCreateHtml`, the Create / Choose-a-template sheets, `copyWorkout`, `createWorkoutFromPastSession`, `_createPersonalWorkout` shared with `saveRunnerAsWorkout` in `js/app-runner.js`); `js/app-programs.js` (`_pgmWeekActionsHtml`, `pgmWeekAction`, and `_assignProgramTx` now sends `p_today`); `js/app-core.js` (`_dateOnly`); 13 date sites across `app-calendar-goals`, `app-programs`, `app-progress`, `app-runner`; `js/app-clients.js` (one `_dateOnly`). No change to row-level security.

## Database changes

Two scripts, both run by Jake on 2026-10-17 BEFORE this code, each proven on a local Postgres first and read back live:

1. `scripts/fix-coach-client-summary-utc-cap-2026-10-17.sql` — the `coach_client_summary` view restated with its two upper date caps `+ 1` (exactly two lines differ from the live view; `security_invoker`, the coach scope, the 19 columns and the grants unchanged). Read-back matched all 29 rows.
2. `scripts/fix-assign-program-restart-date-2026-10-17.sql` — `assign_program()` gets an optional fifth argument `p_today` (clamped to the server's date ± 1; the archive row's `ended_at`); the five-argument function is created first and the four-argument one dropped second. Read-back matched: one function, caller's rights, pinned `search_path`, anon no execute.

Ordering: the SQL went first, which is the safe order: both objects work with the previous browser (v2026.10.4: the new argument is optional). The code in this release REQUIRES the five-argument function; a browser that sends `p_today` to the old function is refused and no plan could be assigned.
