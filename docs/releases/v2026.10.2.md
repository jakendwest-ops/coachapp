## Scope

Jake's six reports of 2026-10-12, answered from a prototype and clickable questions, batched into one release as he chose ("batch"). Items 1 to 4 were
built and committed earlier in the day; items 5 and 6 were built in two steps (plan add/remove, then the Workouts page and the rename).

**In:**
- **The streak counts sessions logged in a row** (it said 16 weeks for someone who had not been on the plan that long). It starts with the plan, and a planned session left unlogged breaks it.
- **Performance tables show recorded effort** (RPE or RIR), the Per-plan tab shows each block's average effort (RIR converted to RPE), and the runner and tables read old effortless sets as RPE ("@7 RPE").
- **Day headings read "WEDNESDAY"**, not "DAY 3" (it was the weekday, not the day of the plan).
- **The client profile no longer flickers between tabs** (a tab race), and the os-lint self-test no longer opens console windows (tooling).
- **"On my plan", Add to / Remove from my plan, one plan at a time** (solo): a chip on the Training plans list and the plan's page; Remove keeps every logged session and takes the plan's upcoming sessions off the calendar; adding a plan takes the other off. Coaches get the same wording for a client's plan.
- **The solo Workouts page:** two start buttons ("Start an empty workout", "Start from a past session"), "Up next" only while a plan is on, the plan folded away, "Your workouts" (workouts you made and used; a plan session joins once done), then Recent sessions. "Save as a workout" on the finish screen is the only way a session becomes reusable, and only when tapped.
- **"Programs" is now "Training plans"** everywhere it is read (nav, headings, buttons, dialogs, toasts, coach screens). Code, table and route names are unchanged.

**Deliberately out:**
- Two plans at once; saving every session automatically (Jake's decisions, `docs/decisions.md`, 2026-10-12).
- A changed Workouts page for a coached client: it is unchanged apart from the words.
- Rewriting how "Your workouts" reads usage (see Known issues).

## What shipped

Plain English first. **Your Programs page is now "Training plans".** A plan you are on shows "On my plan"; its page has "Remove from my plan", which asks first and tells you your logged sessions stay. Adding a different plan takes the old one off and tells you which. **The Workouts page** now opens with the two ways to start a workout that are not part of a plan, your plan's next session, and a list of the workouts you have made and used. Finishing a session offers "Save as a workout". **The streak** counts sessions in a row, and the performance tables show how hard sets were.

Technical: `app-programs.js` (`removeFromMyPlan`, `_otherPlansOf`, `_myPlanStarts`, the sweep in `saveAssignProgramToClient` gated on the id being the viewer's own solo record, row-checked removals through `_removeAssignmentAndClones`), `app-workouts.js` (`renderClientWorkoutsPage` solo layout, `startEmptyWorkout`, `showStartFromPast` and `startFromPastSession` with an in-memory stand-in template that `_startFreshRunner` never turns into a template id, `_yourWorkoutsHtml` with weekly copies of a periodised plan folded into one row), `app-runner.js` (`saveRunnerAsWorkout`: inserts the account's own personal workout and its exercises, removes or reuses the empty workout if the exercises fail, re-entry guarded; a session's `template_id` is now a uuid or nothing, and a failed log insert says so), the visible-wording rename across the nine modules, and `.wk-*`/`.pgm-onplan` styles. No new table, no SQL.

## Known issues carried into this release

- **Several older stacked plans:** an account that already holds more than one assignment shows "On my plan" on each until the next Add sweeps them; removing the newest brings the previous one back as the current plan.
- **"Your workouts" reads the account's sessions once per page open** (paginated, refuses past 10,000 rows): fine today, the thing to revisit if a solo account logs for years.
- **A saved interval or cardio workout keeps no block definition** (only strength-style sets are saved); Back to workout then saving again makes a second workout.
- **On a phone the bottom bar label "Training plans" wraps to two lines.**
- Everything from `v2026.09.14` on still awaits Jake's look on a phone (the `fixed-awaiting-jake` rows in `docs/bugs/`).
