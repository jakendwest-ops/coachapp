## What shipped

Plain English first.
- **Coaches:** the client list can be searched, filtered by status and sorted by who needs attention, and each row shows the client's plan
  and week. Opening a client leads with a summary of where they are. A logged session shows the plan's target next to what was done.
- **Everyone:** the app asks you to agree to the updated privacy policy once. Nothing else about privacy changes: error reports still
  never hold names, emails or health values.
- **Jake (owner):** error reports now say which device, installed app or browser, online or offline, which page and view, and for
  failures that are not normal errors, what kind of value failed. Five blank reports from his iPhone on 2026-10-31 started this.
- **Personal view:** deleting a week from your own programme now moves your plan's later weeks up with it, and rebuilding weeks no
  longer files a false error report. If a renumber is ever refused again, the app says "only partly deleted" instead of claiming success.

Technical:
- Error capture (`js/app-core.js`): `_reportEnv()`, `_reportValueType()`; handler reports include their detail in the dedupe signature;
  an extension's host is never stored; the error listener survives a throwing `stack` getter. New column `app_errors.env`.
- `js/app-progress.js`: owner card "latest: page · role · env" line; export adds `role` and `env`; Send feedback gains a `Device:` line.
- `PRIVACY_POLICY_VERSION` 2026-10-31 (checks.sh 9e pairs it with the page date). The three E2E accounts were re-stamped through the app's
  own consent write, so the suite does not stop at the gate.
- `deletePhaseWeek` (`js/app-programs.js`): both renumbers `.select('id')` with `expectRows`.
- Coach Experience's three items: see their commit messages (`4a13e5d`, `527f0bf`, `0674f74`). No SQL in them.
