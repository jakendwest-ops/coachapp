## Scope

Jake's reports of 2026-10-13, each scoped with him from a prototype or clickable questions and built in his order: the Workouts page, the Streak tile and the
My Progress page ("the whole page needs a UI rework"). The Progress page is reworked in steps; this release has the first two tabs.

**In:**
- **"Your workouts" rows open in place** like a plan day (no slide-in drawer), and the Workouts item in the bottom bar is a dumbbell (it was a cup of coffee).
- **The form classes `.form-label` and `.form-input` are styled** (they had no CSS anywhere, so every form that used them rendered as raw browser controls).
- **My Progress > Body Weight, reworked:** a goal card (start, now, goal, the change), a log sheet (a stepper and the extras folded under "More"), and the entries listed by month. A weight outside 20 to 300 kg is refused with a message.
- **My Progress > Personal Bests, reworked:** one row per lift and a page for each lift (the best history, the best sets by reps, the estimated 1RM trend); recording and editing a best moves onto that page. The 1RM list is read in pages (the API silently caps a response at 200 rows).
- **The Streak tile measures something:** with a plan on, "9 of 10 planned so far", a bar and "Week 3 of 8 · this week 2 of 4"; with none, weeks in a row at a weekly target you set in Settings (1 to 14, default 3), "4 weeks in a row · at 3 a week · this week 1 of 3".
- A browser test of the chart-teardown rule had been red on master since the Personal Bests commit (a push runs no browser tests); it now accepts the lift page's scoped teardown.

**Deliberately out:**
- The Goals tab (cramped, progress unclear, milestones clumsy): prototyped, awaiting Jake's reaction; the next release.
- The week strip, the plan tile, push reminders (the Streak rework left them as they were).
- Fixing the dates that are defaulted from the UTC day (`docs/bugs/2026-10-13-dates-defaulted-from-the-utc-day-are-wrong-outside-utc.md`): scoped, awaiting Jake's go.

## What shipped

Plain English first. **Workouts:** tapping one of your workouts opens its exercises right there, as a plan day does, and the bar icon is a dumbbell. **Body weight:** the page opens
on a card that shows where you started, where you are and where you are headed, with one button to log a weigh-in; the list is grouped by month. **Personal bests:** each lift is
a row; tap it for that lift's whole story, and record or change a best from there. **Streak:** on a plan it tells you how many of the sessions due so far you have done; without
one, how many weeks in a row you hit the number of sessions you chose. The number is chosen in Settings > Weekly target.

Technical: `app-workouts.js` (`_wkExerciseBlockHtml`, `toggleYourWorkout`), `app-core.js` (the dumbbell icon, `_loadWeeklyTarget` started beside the profile read and never in the main
profile select, `_saveWeeklyTarget` asking for the written row back), `app-progress.js` (Body Weight and Personal Bests blocks, `openLiftPage`, `_bestSetsByReps`, the Settings card),
`app-dashboard.js` (`_dashPlanAdherence`, `_dashTargetStreak`, `_dashStreakModel`, `_dashStreakTileHtml`; `_dashStreak` is gone), `app-clients.js` (`saveClientWeight` range), `css/main.css`
(`.wk-*`, `.pw-*`, `.pb-*`, the form class aliases; tokens only).

## Database changes

`scripts/add-weekly-session-target-2026-10-13.sql`: one nullable column, `profiles.weekly_session_target smallint check (between 1 and 14)`, nothing backfilled, no policy or grant.
Applied by Jake in the Supabase SQL editor BEFORE this tag, so the Settings card saves from the first load. The code works without it (the target is read on its own and a failed read is
the default 3), but the card's Save would fail until it is run. Proven locally first: idempotent, 1 to 14 only.
