## Scope

Four things, three of them from Jake's own reports: his 2026-10-17 phone check ("the ghost text in the runner is not always correct"), his 2026-10-25 gym report (since the iOS update every runner sound stopped his music in Spotify, and he said beeps are fine), and the data-leak audit he asked for ("tighten the whole codebase so prevent any data leaks or PII leaks", plan approved first).

**In:**
- **Runner, last-session numbers:** the grey ghost text no longer stops at the client's 20 newest sessions, and a newer session that holds the exercise with no sets no longer hides the real history. A session from a year ago reads with its year.
- **Runner, sound:** the spoken countdown is gone. A low beep at 10 seconds, a short tick for each of the last 5 seconds of a rest or interval (the last 3 of a timed set, each count-in number from 4), and the existing long beep at the end. Nothing in the runner calls the phone's speech engine any more. What you give up: you no longer HEAR which second it is, the ticks are identical and you count them. A tick that could only sound late (the audio was interrupted by a call or another app) is dropped rather than sounding the wrong second, and a tap wakes an interrupted audio context as well as a suspended one.
- **Two reads tied to their owner:** the coach dashboard's "Goals due soon" no longer lists the master account's own personal goals, and the Personal calendar no longer lists the master account's coaching clients' events.
- **Leak and PII gates (nothing visible in the app):** five push checks for secrets, definer functions, outside hosts, PII in logs and query scope, each with a self-test and a mutation run; `invite-client`'s source is now in the repo and the gate that reads it follows the key to where it is used.

**Deliberately out:**
- Rows still matched by set number with warm-ups included, and today's prescription still winning over last session's numbers (named in the ghost-text ledger row; Jake has not said which case he saw).
- The `invite-client` fixes (an unpinned library import, unchecked follow-up writes, 400 for an unauthorised call): the deployed copy is edited in the Supabase dashboard, so they wait for Jake. Open ledger row.
- Self-hosting Google Fonts, a Content-Security-Policy, export/erase parity: later stretches of the audit.

## What shipped

Plain English first. **In the gym:** the rest timer, the interval timer, the timed set and the get-ready count-in now make beeps instead of speaking, so a song playing in Spotify carries on. You hear a lower beep when 10 seconds are left, then a tick each second at the end, then the long beep. **Ghost text:** the numbers from your last session of an exercise show even if you last did it a while ago. **Dashboard and calendar:** the master account stops seeing things that belong to its other role.

Technical: `js/app-runner.js` (`playCountdownTick`, `playTenSecondWarning` replace `speakCue`; `_unlockSpeech` and `_pickFemaleVoice` deleted; `_unlockSpeech()` removed from nine call sites in `js/app-runner.js` and `js/app-workouts.js`; `fetchRunnerLastSession` asks the database for the newest sessions that hold the exercise, joined to the session with `workout_logs!inner(date, client_id)`, filtered by the client and ordered by date; `_runnerShortDate` adds the year past about ten months); `js/app-dashboard.js` (the goals read joins `clients!inner` and filters on `coach_id`); `js/app-calendar-goals.js` (the Personal calendar's `events` read is filtered to its own record). Gates: `scripts/check-secrets.mjs`, `check-sql-definer.mjs`, `check-external-surface.mjs`, `check-pii-logs.mjs`, `check-query-scope.mjs`, `scripts/mutate-gates.mjs`. New specs: `tests/runner-last-session-window-2026-10-18.spec.js`, `tests/dashboard-goals-scoped-2026-10-18.spec.js`, `tests/calendar-events-scoped-2026-10-18.spec.js`, `tests/client-link-guard-2026-10-18.spec.js`, `tests-node/runner-cues-are-tones.test.mjs`. ## Database changes

Three scripts, each run by Jake in the SQL editor on 2026-10-25, each proven on a local Postgres with a deliberate-break suite first and read back live. They were applied BEFORE this code, and nothing in this code depends on them (the browser works with or without them):

1. `scripts/add-client-link-guard-2026-10-18.sql` — one invoker trigger function and a `BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE OF client_id` trigger on `events`, `goals` and `workout_templates`, so a coach cannot link a row to another coach's client. Read-back: 1 function (`security_definer=false`), 3 triggers.
2. `scripts/harden-anon-access-2026-10-18.sql` — every privilege of the signed-out role on the app's tables and sequences revoked, default privileges for new objects, and nine functions closed to it; `delete_current_user()` is granted to signed-in users explicitly. Read-back: 0 tables, 0 sequences, only `mock_openapi` callable, signed-in users unchanged. A signed-out request now answers 401 instead of an empty list.
3. `scripts/pin-trigger-search-path-2026-10-25.sql` — `search_path` pinned on two trigger functions the Supabase Security Advisor flagged. Read-back: both set.

Also done in the dashboard, not in code: the minimum password length is 10; the leaked-password check is not available on the current plan.
