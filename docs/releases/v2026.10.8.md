## Scope

One request from Jake on 2026-10-28, after he saw the workout-editor prototype render dark because his phone is set to dark: "i also prefer the dark mode, could we make that the default across the app" ([row](../bugs/2026-10-28-dark-mode-should-be-the-default-across-the-app.md)). He gave the go on 2026-10-29 and chose to keep the choice on the phone rather than on the account.

**In:**
- **Dark is the default theme** for everyone who has not chosen: a new account, a signed-out visitor, the login page, the privacy page and the 404 redirect. The palette is the one from the editor prototype he liked.
- **Settings > Appearance** (every role): Dark, Light or Match my phone. It applies the moment it changes, is remembered on this device (localStorage `coachapp-theme`), and Match my phone follows the phone live. Light is today's palette, value for value (a test pins every token).
- A head script applies the saved choice before the first paint, so there is no flash of the wrong theme.

**Deliberately out:**
- Remembering the choice on the account: it would need a database column (SQL from Jake) and cannot apply on the login page. A device-only choice was Jake's pick; the account version can still be added.
- Chart colours. They were already read from the stylesheet when a chart is built, and the metric and category colours are deliberate mid-tones that read on both themes.
- A "light" version of the new deeper green and amber fills: Light keeps `#10b981` and `#f59e0b` exactly.

## What shipped

Plain English first. Open the app and it is dark; Settings has a new Appearance card with three choices. Nothing else about how the app works changed. If you pick Light you get the app exactly as it looked before (with four tiny exceptions listed under Known issues).

Technical:
- `css/main.css`: the colour tokens in `:root` are the dark values; `:root[data-theme="light"]` holds the old values. New tokens: `--on-accent` (text on an accent fill: dark ink in dark, white in light), `--success-fg`, `--danger-text`, `--success-text`, `--warning-text`, `--border-strong`, `--accent-border`, `--placeholder`, `--badge-neutral-bg`, `--danger-hover-bg`, `--auth-from` / `--auth-to`. In dark, `--success` and `--warning` are deeper fills so white on them reaches 3:1 (white on `#10b981` was 2.5:1 in the runner's tick button).
- `index.html`, `privacy-policy.html`, `404.html`: the head script, `color-scheme` and `theme-color` meta tags; the privacy page has its own small light and dark palette.
- `js/app-core.js`: `getThemeChoice`, `applyTheme`, `setThemeChoice` and a live `matchMedia` follow; a stored value outside `dark | light | system` reads as dark. `js/app-progress.js`: the Appearance card. About 60 swaps across the modules: white text on accent fills became `var(--on-accent)`, red text became `var(--danger-text)`, green text `var(--success-fg)`, and a few literal pale surfaces and dark text shades became tokens.
- `scripts/style-baseline.json`: re-pinned at the new measurements (css 111 to 144, the new token definitions; the six JS counts fell).
- Module versions: app-core 49, app-dashboard 38, app-programs 79, app-clients 26, app-calendar-goals 34, app-workouts 152, app-runner 124, app-progress 88, main.css 40.

## Known issues carried into this release

- **Light is not byte-identical in four places**, because a one-off literal colour became the nearest token: the amber note text (`#b45309` now `#92400e`, in the %1RM notice and the runner's "Set your 1RM" prompt), the 1RM calculator's result box (pale green box and `#15803d` text now the success tokens), and the `is-good` pill (a computed green now `#065f46`). All are small shifts in shade.
- **Contrast between 3:1 and 4.5:1 is not asserted.** A few things sit there on purpose or by history: the metric and category colours (indigo `#6366f1` values at about 3.8:1), red text (about 4.5 to 6:1), and `.xs-pill` selected text on its accent fallback (about 3.1:1).
- **A chart already on screen keeps the old colours** if "Match my phone" flips while it is open; it picks the new ones the next time it is drawn.
- The coach's logo preview sits on the dark surface: a black logo with a transparent background is hard to see there.
- The privacy policy's local-storage sentence still says only "your preferred view"; the theme key is a UI preference with no personal data. Changing the wording bumps the policy version and re-asks every user for consent, so it is left for a deliberate change.
- `2026-10-28-runner-beeps-worked-for-two-exercises-then-were-never-heard-again` (open, reopened): waiting on Jake's round-2 sound test.
- All earlier `fixed-awaiting-jake` rows: each closes only when Jake confirms on his phone. This release moves `2026-10-28-dark-mode-should-be-the-default-across-the-app` to `fixed-awaiting-jake`.
