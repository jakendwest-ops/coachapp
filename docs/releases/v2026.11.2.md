## What shipped

Plain English first.
- **Last week of a plan,** with nothing booked after it: a quiet line on Today, "Last week of your block. Ends Sunday.", with "Plan next block". The running plan stays on; the new one follows it.
- **Once a plan has ended,** with nothing booked: a "Block complete" card on Today for up to 4 weeks. It shows planned sessions done, the three most improved lifts, and body weight when there is a weigh-in near each end. A plan needs at least 2 weeks and 3 logged sessions to earn one.
- **Repeat this plan** opens the sheet with the plan and start date filled in, and one Confirm books it. **Choose another plan** opens the same sheet to pick from. Both take the finished plan off (one plan at a time), and it stays in the Blocks history. A plan booked for later is never taken off.
- **See everything** opens Blocks frozen on the finished block against the one before it, under a header with the same figures as the card. It lasts one visit.
- **Not now** hides the card until next Monday, on this device.

Technical: `_dashPlanEndWeek`, `_dashPlanEndSunday`, `_blockEndState`, `_blockReviewModel`, `_dashBlockSetup`, `_dashFillBlockCard`, `_dashBlockAction`; `_blockReviewWindows`, `_openBlockReview`, `_blockReviewHeaderHtml`; `showAssignProgramModal` gains `opts.programId` and `opts.blockReview`; `saveAssignProgram` repaints Today and shares `_takeOtherPlansOff` with `saveAssignProgramToClient`. `_dashPlanAdherence` now shares the end-of-plan rule.

## Known issues carried into this release

- After booking, an archived block ends on the day it was replaced, so the normal Blocks page can count days after the plan's last Sunday. The review does not. Acceptable: those windows are labelled by date.
- On a first block the card says there is nothing to compare, while the review shows a labelled stand-in window. Acceptable: both are honest.
- The card's lift data reloads on each Today repaint during its 28 days. Acceptable: bounded, and the same read as the Blocks page.
- "Not now" is per device, as designed.
- The Supabase API log check for these flows was not run (it needs the dashboard).
- GDPR consent capture stays deferred by Jake ([row](../bugs/2026-08-11-gdpr-no-consent-capture-and-no-privacy-policy.md)).
