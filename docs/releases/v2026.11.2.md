## What shipped

Plain English first.
- **Last week of a plan,** with nothing booked after it: a quiet line on Today, "Last week of your block. Ends Sunday.", with "Plan next block". The running plan stays on; the new one follows it.
- **Once a plan has ended,** with nothing booked: a "Block complete" card on Today for up to 4 weeks. It shows planned sessions done, the three most improved lifts, and body weight when there is a weigh-in near each end. A plan needs at least 2 weeks and 3 logged sessions to earn one.
- **Repeat this plan** opens the sheet with the plan and start date filled in, and one Confirm books it. **Choose another plan** opens the same sheet to pick from. Both take the finished plan off (one plan at a time), and it stays in the Blocks history. A plan booked for later is never taken off.
- **See everything** opens Blocks frozen on the finished block against the one before it, under a header with the same figures as the card. It lasts one visit.
- **Not now** hides the card until next Monday, on this device.

- **Sessions compares each workout with last time:** every card adds a line such as "vs Thu 29 Oct: −525 kg · 2 of 3 exercises up", or "First time doing this workout". Tapping a card compares it set by set, with ↑, ↓, "not done" and a verdict per exercise. Bars at the top show sets per week for 8 weeks, with a switch to total kg. A failed history read still shows the latest sessions and says the comparisons could not load (before, a failed read looked like "No sessions logged yet").

- **The coach's phone bar has five items, not six:** Dashboard, Clients, Training plans, Calendar and More. Workouts and Settings are in the More sheet, with Download my data, Send feedback, Privacy policy, Switch to Personal view (only with a Personal record) and Sign out, and More stays lit on them. The desktop sidebar keeps all six; the client's bar and Personal view's are unchanged. Signing out now also clears "View as", so the next person on that tab inherits nothing.

Technical: `_navBottomItems`, `_navLitFor`, `_navView`, `_moreSheetRowsHtml` (the sidebar and the bar each light by their own rule); `_previousSessionOf`, `_setMark`, `_pairSets`, `_sessionCompare`, `_sessionVsLine`, `_weeklyVolume`; `renderProgressPerSession` now reads the whole history through `_fetchAllRows`; `_takeOtherPlansOff` is shared; `_dashPlanEndWeek`, `_dashPlanEndSunday`, `_blockEndState`, `_blockReviewModel`, `_dashBlockSetup`, `_dashFillBlockCard`, `_dashBlockAction`; `_blockReviewWindows`, `_openBlockReview`, `_blockReviewHeaderHtml`; `showAssignProgramModal` gains `opts.programId` and `opts.blockReview`; `saveAssignProgram` repaints Today and shares `_takeOtherPlansOff` with `saveAssignProgramToClient`. `_dashPlanAdherence` now shares the end-of-plan rule.

## Known issues carried into this release

- After booking, an archived block ends on the day it was replaced, so the normal Blocks page can count days after the plan's last Sunday. The review does not. Acceptable: those windows are labelled by date.
- On a first block the card says there is nothing to compare, while the review shows a labelled stand-in window. Acceptable: both are honest.
- The card's lift data reloads on each Today repaint during its 28 days. Acceptable: bounded, and the same read as the Blocks page.
- "Not now" is per device, as designed.
- Sessions now reads the whole history on each open, as the Blocks page does. Live on the test accounts: 100 sessions in 140 ms.
- A bodyweight-only week shows an empty bar in kg, which is true. On the coach's tab, saving a record can leave an expanded session chart blank until the tab is reopened.
- More's Sign out does not warn about an unsaved workout edit (the desktop sign-out button does not either). A failed Download from More shows no message. During "View as" (owner only) the coach's bar stays on screen and Workouts and Settings light nothing.
- "Resend invite" is gone and has no replacement yet ([row](../bugs/2026-10-31-resend-invite-is-always-refused.md)).
- The Supabase API log check for these flows was not run (it needs the dashboard).
- GDPR consent capture stays deferred by Jake ([row](../bugs/2026-08-11-gdpr-no-consent-capture-and-no-privacy-policy.md)).
