# v2026.10.4 — The Goals tab reworked, and solo accounts can add a step to their own goal

**Cut:** 2026-10-15
**Commits:** `v2026.10.3..7c79b05` (the Goals rework, its review fixes, the solo-steps policy and its verifier), plus the notes commits
**Previous release:** v2026.10.3 (2026-10-15, live)

---

## Scope

Jake's report of 2026-10-13 on the Goals tab ("cramped or cluttered, progress unclear, milestones clumsy"), built from his prototype reactions (fuller list cards, no pace pill or weekly line, a step with a number ticks itself), and the open ledger row that a solo user could not add a step to their own goal.

**In:**
- **List:** goals in order of priority, each card showing the number, how far to go, a bar, the next step, the steps count, the last update and the due date; finished goals folded away; a card opens by tap, Enter or Space.
- **A goal's page:** the number first, how far to go, a bar with a mark per step, where it started and is headed; **Update progress** (a number and a note; the date under a fold); **Steps** as a checklist with a one-line add; **History** (the newest five).
- **Steps with a number tick themselves** once progress passes them (derived, never written), only when the number lies on the goal's own line; anything else is ticked by hand.
- **Short new-goal and edit sheets**; the old fields (description, type, priority, metric label, status) folded under More options; "Mark goal finished" / "Reopen goal"; From is editable.
- **Solo accounts can add a step to their own goal** (a database policy; see Database changes).

**Deliberately out:**
- Deleting a step (nothing in the app does; the new policy grants INSERT only).
- The pace pill and the "kg a week" line (Jake's call).
- The UTC-date fix (`docs/bugs/2026-10-13-dates-defaulted-from-the-utc-day-are-wrong-outside-utc.md`), still awaiting his go.

## What shipped

Plain English first. **Goals:** the list is a stack of cards that each tell you where you stand and what is next; tap one for the goal's own page, where the big number is how far along you are, "Update progress" is one number and one note, and the steps are a checklist you can add to in one line. A step like "Squat 140 kg" ticks itself once you pass 140. **Solo:** in Personal view, adding a step to your own goal now works (it was refused by the database before).

Technical: `js/app-calendar-goals.js` (the pure goal model `_goalNums` / `_goalFrac` / `_goalStepReached` / `_goalNextStep` / `_goalTimeLeft`; `renderClientGoals`, `goalCard`, `openGoal`, `addStep`, `showAddCheckInModal` / `saveCheckIn`, `showAddGoalModal` / `saveNewGoal`, `showEditGoalModal` / `saveEditGoal`, `_repaintGoal`); `saveNewGoal`, `saveNewMilestone`, `saveCheckIn`, `addStep` and `toggleMilestone` are re-entry guarded; the goal's number update and the tick are row-counted; `css/main.css` (`.gl-*`, tokens only).

## Database changes

`scripts/add-solo-goal-milestones-policy-2026-10-15.sql`: ONE new policy on `goal_milestones`, FOR INSERT to authenticated, WITH CHECK the step's goal belongs to the caller's own solo record (`coach_id IS NULL` and `user_id = auth.uid()`). Additive (a permissive policy can only grant), INSERT only. Applied by Jake in the Supabase SQL editor BEFORE this tag. Proven locally on PGlite loaded with the live policies Jake read on 2026-10-15 (40 checks, 9 breakages caught), and against the live database by `tests/solo-goal-milestones-2026-10-15.spec.js` (red before, green after). The code works without it (the refusal is shown under the box); a solo user just cannot add a step until it is run.

## Known issues carried into this release

- **Jake has not looked at any of this on a phone yet** (the `fixed-awaiting-jake` rows, including the Streak tile, Body Weight, Personal Bests and now Goals).
- **A client who detaches themselves from their coach becomes a solo record** and can then add steps to the goals their coach made (the detach is already accepted; pinned in the verifier).
- **The dashboard's percentage and the card's differ** for a goal with a start and a target but no current number (the dashboard counts steps, the card shows 0%).
- **A step's number is not range-checked when typed**, and a step cannot be deleted.
- **Test-suite timing (found cutting this release):** a slow Supabase evening exposed that the specs' `soloView` helpers returned before the dashboard had painted, so a late paint overwrote the page a test had mounted (the Goals scroll test failed twice; fixed in `goals-rework` and `form-classes-styled`, reproduced and proven first). `tests/pb-consolidation-2026-08-17.spec.js` still uses four fixed sleeps (2.5 s and 1.5 s) for the same overwrite; they have passed every full suite so far.
- **The UTC-date class (open):** a session logged 00:00 to 01:00 BST dates to the day before; 22 specs build "today" from the UTC date.
